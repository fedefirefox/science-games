# Institutional branding

The project owner, a SLAC employee, explicitly approved adding the SLAC logo for this educational release on 5 November 2026. This records the owner's instruction; it does not claim separate approval from SLAC Communications.

The official white SLAC logo is bundled unchanged from the public SLAC website. It appears on a dark background with generous clear space, its natural aspect ratio, a height of 52 CSS pixels and descriptive alternative text. No institutional marks are combined into a new logo. LCLS is identified through a separate text link to its official website.

The logo is excluded from the MIT software license; see [NOTICE.md](NOTICE.md). Reusers must obtain any required authorization for their own use. This project does not purport to grant trademark rights or institutional endorsement.

Official guidance and source:
- https://www6.slac.stanford.edu/about/resources/logos
- https://www6.slac.stanford.edu/themes/gesso/dist/images/SLAC_Logo_W.svg

No logos were redrawn, recolored, distorted or hotlinked. No permission request was sent on the owner's behalf.
