Scope: LCLS TMO lists distinct nominal minimum X-ray separations: about 0.3 fs for harmonic modes and 10 fs for arbitrary colors. Do not apply a 10 fs SXR1–e⁻ planning offset to the harmonic case. Also, SXRSS ordinarily denotes soft-X-ray self-seeding, not the generic two-color split-undulator configuration. The fixed 5 GeV beam and U1 = 9 are example configuration assumptions. The TMO page gives differing energy ranges in its overview (250–1800 eV) and table (200–2000 eV); confirm operating availability with the facility.
